# About Encore

Mode: Read

Audience and job: A prospective or returning concertgoer who needs a plain-language explanation of how Encore forms a likely live-set playlist, what evidence they can inspect, and the limits of current streaming support.

Action: Read the evidence model, then start a playlist or return to the app.

Proof and constraints: Use only recent public Setlist.fm history, confidence, appearance counts, covers, and provider facts established in PRODUCT.md. Spotify is a beta integration while production access is pending; Apple Music is fully supported. Do not use provider branding as ambient decoration or imply availability that has not shipped.

Direction: Field-guide narrative. The first viewport is a prose introduction paired with a compact evidence ledger. The page then moves from evidence to an export roadmap to a quiet FAQ; it remains strongly readable instead of becoming a feature-card grid. On mobile, the account drawer presents an Explore group before Account.
